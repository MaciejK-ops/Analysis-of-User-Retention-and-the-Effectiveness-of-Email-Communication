# Analysis of User Retention & Email Communication Effectiveness

![Project Banner](assets/dashboard_preview.png) *(Optional: Replace with your dashboard screenshot)*

## 📌 Business Overview & Objective
Understanding user engagement and retention is crucial for optimizing product growth and marketing strategies. This project analyzes user account creation dynamics and email interaction metrics (sent, opened, and clicked) to uncover behavioral patterns across key dimensions.

### Core Objectives:
* **Account Creation Dynamics:** Track growth trends and account verification behaviors.
* **Email Engagement:** Evaluate campaign effectiveness by analyzing open rates (OR), click-through rates (CTR), and sending intervals.
* **Subscription Status & Verification:** Examine how account verification and subscription preferences impact retention.
* **Geographic Analysis:** Compare user engagement across countries to identify high-converting key markets.
* **User Segmentation:** Categorize users into distinct segments based on activity levels, geo-location, and subscription attributes.

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Account Creation & Verification Status
* Tracking signup trends over time.
* Analyzing the ratio of verified vs. unverified user accounts across regions.

### 2. Email Activity & Interval Analysis
* Volume metrics: Total emails sent, opened, and clicked.
* Time-gap analysis between consecutive emails and user interactions.

### 3. Regional & Segment Insights
* Top performing countries by total accounts and high engagement rates.
* Cohort and segment breakdown by subscription preference and verification level.

## 💡 Summary of Key Findings
* **Top Country Engagement:** United States and India represent the largest user base by total account volume.
* **Verification Impact:** Verified accounts demonstrate significantly higher email open and click rates compared to unverified profiles.
* **Sending Cadence:** Email open rates decline when the interval between consecutive emails falls below 24 hours.
